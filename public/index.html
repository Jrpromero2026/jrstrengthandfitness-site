The standard underneath

Match the
intervention
to the person.

IAOE runs through everything I build: coaching, training programs, nutrition, trainer development and the client experience. Adequate is a legitimate target, not a failure.

Who coaches the person, and at what level.

01Below the lineInadequateUnsafe, unstructured or not progressing.
02Fitness trainerAdequateSafe, effective and progressing. The right target for many clients.
03Fitness coachOptimalIndividualized programming built around the person’s goals.
04Performance coachEnhancedWhere I coach: trained athletes who have plateaued and want finer margins.

What the plan on paper actually does.

01RandomInadequateWorkouts with no plan: no progression, no reason behind exercise choice, injury risk left to chance.
02StructuredAdequateProgressive overload, balanced movement patterns and safe technique. Enough for most people to keep progressing.
03IndividualizedOptimalBuilt around the person’s goals, history, schedule and limitations, with planned phases and adjustments.
04Periodized for marginsEnhancedAdvanced periodization and specific methods for trained athletes chasing the last few percent.

How the eating supports the training.

01No planInadequateUnder-fueled or unstructured eating that works against the training.
02Consistent basicsAdequateEnough protein, enough total energy and regular meals to support training and recovery.
03TargetsOptimalCalorie and macro targets matched to the goal and training load, adjusted as progress comes in.
04Precision fuelingEnhancedTiming, periodized intake and competition fueling where small margins matter.

How coaches are qualified, educated and held to a standard.

01UnverifiedInadequateCredentials nobody checked, no continuing education and no standard to coach to.
02AccreditedAdequateAn accredited certification and safe, effective sessions. A legitimate place to start.
03DevelopingOptimalOngoing education, mentorship and real systems: consultations, programming frameworks and client tools.
04SpecializedEnhancedAdvanced credentials and specialization, such as performance coaching for athletes.

What it feels like to be the client, from first contact on.

01Ad hocInadequateNo consult, no onboarding and no follow-up. Clients drift and leave.
02ConsistentAdequateA real consult, a clear start and regular check-ins.
03CoordinatedOptimalIntake, programming, scheduling and check-ins in one system, so nothing falls through.
04ProactiveEnhancedReadiness data, reviews and adjustments made before problems show up.

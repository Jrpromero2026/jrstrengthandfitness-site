What it is

A coach-built training app for women. Programs are organized into phases, weeks and workouts, with a target for every set, technique videos for every lift, and nutrition that matches the training.

What’s inside
  • Coach-built programs organized into phases, weeks and workouts
  • Set-by-set logging with suggested targets and coach cues
  • Demo videos and the reasoning behind every exercise
  • Calorie and macro targets with meal logging
  • Progress tracking: body weight, strength, measurements, PRs and private photos
  • The Built For Her™ Score: training, fuel and standards over seven days
From the floor

Why I built it

Most fitness apps for women are content libraries. Built For Her™ is a training system: the same structure, progression and coaching standard I use with clients on the floor, built into an app.

JR Prieto-Romero, CSCS

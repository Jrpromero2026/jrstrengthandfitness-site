How you learn it

Every concept, three ways.

  • Repetition. Flashcard review until it sticks.
  • Categorization. Sort sets that make you place each idea where it belongs.
  • Association. Scenarios that connect one concept to the next.

Knowledge is tiered the way I coach: Inadequate, Adequate, Optimal and Enhanced. The 1,022 exam-standard questions are the core. The Optimal and Enhanced questions are there when you want more.

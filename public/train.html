Questions

Before you
book.

Straight answers about consults, facilities and online coaching.

How do I start personal training with JR in Corvallis?

Book a free 30-minute consult at Timberhill Athletic Club or G3 Sports & Fitness. We cover your goals, training history, limitations, barriers and preferences, then I match you with the right coach, facility and program. Sometimes that coach isn’t me.

Do I need a gym membership to book a consult?

No. You don’t need to be a member of Timberhill or G3 to book a free consult.

Should I train at Timberhill Athletic Club or G3 Sports & Fitness?

Timberhill is built around health, general fitness and longevity for adults at every starting point. G3 focuses on youth athletic development, adult human performance, and tactical, team and organization training. Not sure? Pick either one; one consult covers both.

Can I train with JR if I don’t live in Corvallis?

Yes. Online coaching gives you individualized training and nutrition through the coaching app, with check-ins and adjustments. It starts with an application: I reply within 2 business days, and if it’s a fit we do a short call before you get your app invite.

How much does online coaching cost?

Essentials is $199 a month, Core is $249 a month and Premium is $319 a month. Three- and six-month terms lower the monthly rate. Every package includes both training and nutrition coaching.

Do you train beginners?

Yes. Plenty of my clients had never worked with a trainer. We start with technique and a plan you can follow on your own schedule.

Can you help me get strong again after an injury?

Yes. Once rehab is done, I build a plan around the injury that rebuilds strength and confidence, progressed as you’re ready.

Do you train high school and college athletes?

Yes, at G3 Sports & Fitness: strength, speed and conditioning for athletes heading into college sport. Athletes I’ve coached have gone on to play at the University of Oregon, the University of Nevada, George Fox, Houston Christian, Saint Anselm and Eastern Illinois.

Where are the sessions?

In person at Timberhill Athletic Club, 2855 NW 29th St, or G3 Sports & Fitness, 5520 NW Highway 99W, both in Corvallis. Online coaching works anywhere.

Who is JR Romero?

JR Romero, CSCS, is Director of Personal Training at Timberhill Athletic Club and Director of Performance at G3 Sports & Fitness in Corvallis, Oregon. He earned his degree in Exercise & Sport Science at Oregon State and has roughly 20,000 hours of coaching on the floor.
